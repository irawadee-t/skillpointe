Design Language

Cohere-style editorial restraint: austere black-and-white UI, large carved type, flat bordered cards, and bursts of deep-green brand field. Color arrives through brand surfaces and small coral/blue accents — not decorative chrome.

Editorial restraint
White canvas is the default; deep green / navy appear as full-width bands and bold cards. One oversized headline per view, then calm 16–24px copy.
Flat, not floaty
Depth = surface alternation (white / stone / green) + thin hairlines + rounded corners. No heavy drop shadows.
Readable first
Secondary text is slate #52525b (not whisper-grey); primary info ≥ 16px; mono labels 13px medium. Numbers are big display tabular-nums.
Honest ranking
Hard-gate failures and gaps are surfaced, never hidden in an opaque score. Geography is always visible.

Do

  • Use deep green (#003c33) boldly on KPI cards & hero/summary panels — white text.
  • Keep primary CTAs near-black pills; secondary as underlined links.
  • 22px radius on major media; 8–16px on cards/chips.
  • Coral for taxonomy / near-fit; blue for links; green for eligible / strength.
  • Wide containers + dense data on dashboards; narrow reading column on forms/threads.
  • Mono uppercase labels for sections & system markers.

Don't

  • Don't drown the page in tiny grey text or empty whitespace.
  • Don't add heavy shadows or make every section a boxed card.
  • Don't turn coral/blue into broad surface fills.
  • Don't stretch forms to full width — inputs look bad.
  • Don't collapse the display/body/mono split into one generic sans.
  • Don't put dark text on green — always white / white-opacity.
